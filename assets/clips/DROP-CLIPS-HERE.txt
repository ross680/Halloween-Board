Halloween clips live here (MP4, WebM, MOV, or images).
They play in file-name order during the Halloween window, Oct 1 through Nov 1.
Number them 01-, 02-, … so the order is obvious.
Extra Halloween files can also go in assets/themes/halloween/.
Commit the files. GitHub Actions rebuilds clips.json and slides.js.
To rebuild them yourself: node scripts/update-manifest.mjs
